
The 40-Expression Core Database


Module 1: The American Hierarchy & Softening Translator (Universal)

Targeting the universal fear of sounding aggressive, demanding, or disrespectful to a Western boss or international client.
1. The Raw Thought: "You must sign this contract today."
	• The Problem: Too demanding; sounds like an ultimatum.
2. The Raw Thought: "I don't understand your point. Explain it again."
	• The Problem: Sounds accusatory, as if the listener is being dense.
3. The Raw Thought: "Your idea is impossible for our team."
	• The Problem: Too dismissive; shuts down collaboration instantly.
4. The Raw Thought: "You forgot to send the attachment."
	• The Problem: Directly blames the client instead of focusing on the missing file.
5. The Raw Thought: "I need you to help me fix this right now."
	• The Problem: Entitled tone; ignores the other person's schedule.
6. The Raw Thought: "That is wrong. The data says something else."
	• The Problem: Creates a confrontational atmosphere rather than a constructive discussion.
7. The Raw Thought: "I don't care which option we pick."
	• The Problem: Sounds disengaged and passive rather than flexible.
8. The Raw Thought: "Give me feedback on my work."
	• The Problem: Sounds like a blunt command rather than a professional request for guidance.
9. The Raw Thought: "You didn't answer my question in the email."
	• The Problem: Accusatory; implies the other party is avoiding the topic or being lazy.
10. The Raw Thought: "I will talk to you later when I am less busy."
	• The Problem: Sounds dismissive and borderline rude.

Module 2: The Conflict & Pushback Engine (South Korea Silo)

Targeting the anxiety of defying vertical hierarchy, saying no to a senior stakeholder, or delivering bad news (Nunchi protection).
11. The Raw Thought: "We cannot launch tomorrow. The server is completely broken."
	• The Problem: Panic-inducing; lacks an immediate proactive solution.
12. The Raw Thought: "I cannot work this weekend for your emergency."
	• The Problem: Too personal; sounds defensive rather than objective.
13. The Raw Thought: "The client is making a big mistake with this request."
	• The Problem: Violates corporate face-saving protocols by declaring a partner wrong.
14. The Raw Thought: "I am too busy with other work to do this task."
	• The Problem: Sounds uncooperative in team-oriented Korean office cultures.
15. The Raw Thought: "Our team didn't meet the target because of marketing."
	• The Problem: Shifts blame directly onto another department, which is poorly received in Western structures.
16. The Raw Thought: "Your budget is way too small for this project."
	• The Problem: Bluntly rejects financial terms without providing a middle-ground proposal.
17. The Raw Thought: "I don't know the answer to this issue."
	• The Problem: Terrifying for a manager to say out loud; sounds incompetent without framing.
18. The Raw Thought: "Stop changing the project requirements every week."
	• The Problem: Aggressive frustration directed at a paying client or manager.
19. The Raw Thought: "We need to delay the contract signing."
	• The Problem: Disrupts timeline expectations without giving a strong justification.
20. The Raw Thought: "I disagree with my manager's conclusion on this report."
	• The Problem: High-stakes internal conflict; requires incredibly delicate framing.

Module 3: The Global Zoom Meeting Survival Anchor (Japan Silo)

Targeting the extreme paralysis Japanese professionals face when trying to break out of silence, slow down fast native speakers, or buy time to formulate perfect thoughts.
21. The Target Intent: I need to break my silence and add an idea right now.
22. The Target Intent: The American team is speaking way too fast; I need them to slow down.
23. The Target Intent: I need 30 seconds to check my Japanese data sheet before I answer.
24. The Target Intent: I want to ask a question, but everyone keeps talking over me.
25. The Target Intent: I didn't understand the idiom or corporate slang they just used.
26. The Target Intent: I need to hand the microphone over to my colleague now.
27. The Target Intent: I want to bring the conversation back to the main agenda topic.
28. The Target Intent: Someone asked me a sudden question, and my mind went completely blank.
29. The Target Intent: I want to confirm if everyone can see my shared screen before starting.
30. The Target Intent: I need to drop off the call early for a domestic client obligation.

Module 4: The Supply Chain & KPI Presenter (Taiwan Silo)

Targeting hardware/software engineers and project managers who are brilliant technically but sound robotic, cluttered, or overly theoretical when delivering data to foreign clients.
31. The Raw Metric Note: "Chip yield went up 4% this month."
	• The Goal: Turn a flat stat into an impactful business growth story.
32. The Raw Metric Note: "TSMC shipment is delayed 7 days because of shipping jam at port."
	• The Goal: Explain a major logistics failure without panicking the overseas buyer.
33. The Raw Metric Note: "The product has a lot of bugs. We need to fix them."
	• The Goal: Describe engineering roadblocks without underselling the team's capabilities.
34. The Raw Metric Note: "Look at this slide. The line goes up."
	• The Goal: Elevate weak visual storytelling into strong corporate data analysis.
35. The Raw Metric Note: "We changed the factory plan to save some money."
	• The Goal: Frame cost-cutting or cost-reduction strategies as proactive optimization.
36. The Raw Metric Note: "This is the final summary of the whole project."
	• The Goal: Wrap up a high-stakes presentation with an active call to action, avoiding an anti-climactic drop.
37. The Raw Metric Note: "We don't have enough materials to make the hardware next month."
	• The Goal: Flag a critical capacity bottleneck while presenting proactive backup options.
38. The Raw Metric Note: "We tested the software and it passed the minimum level."
	• The Goal: Shift defensive engineering jargon into confident quality assurance verification.
39. The Raw Metric Note: "The cost of copper is up, so the price might change."
	• The Goal: Prepare global clients for incoming price adjustments due to raw material macroeconomics.
40. The Raw Metric Note: "I am going to explain the long history of our design process."
	• The Goal: Pivot away from text-heavy, time-wasting historical context to protect the attention spans of busy executives.
